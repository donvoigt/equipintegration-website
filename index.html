Our Mission: Energy Efficiency in Industrial and Utility Systems

EEI specializes in energy use reduction in industrial plant and public utility operations, providing optimum system design through cooperative engineering, to achieve safe, secure, and cost efficient systems.

For certified energy audits — Types I, II and III — look to EEI, the energy & systems professionals.

System Optimization

  • Supply cost analysis to support recommendations and find the lowest life cycle expense
  • Compare system alternatives to assist informed decisions
  • Assist owners, operators and engineers in improving system efficiency
  • Design systems that are safe, secure and provide the highest lifetime value

Energy Efficiency Management

In addition to optimizing equipment choices, decision-makers need to focus on energy efficiency. EEI evaluates new and existing systems and suggests improvements to help save operating costs.

Independence for Better Service

As an independent consultant, Don Voigt is not directly affiliated with, nor financially compensated by, any sales organizations or manufacturers. This independence enables objective comparisons of equipment and systems.
